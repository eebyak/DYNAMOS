# DYNAMOS derivation decisions — pilot

## D-001 — RC
RC means **Re-Entry Cost** throughout the canonical model.

The older use of RC as “Regulation Capacity” is retired. Regulation is treated as an emergent process expressed through specific DYNAMOS mechanisms and non-dimensional regulation loops.

## D-002 — Scale
The 1–10 questionnaire is interpreted as a **comparative self-estimate**: the respondent estimates their typical functioning relative to what they perceive as typical among other adults.

The arrows are shorthand for those self-estimated bands, not population percentiles.

## D-003 — PRECEPTA derivation roles
The Cognitive & Learning pilot distinguishes:
- core conditions
- supporting conditions
- modifiers
- context-sensitive conditions
- patterns that are not directly inferable from the current 26 items

These role assignments are provisional model-specification decisions for empirical testing.

## D-004 — Fast Insight / Slow Explanation
The current 26 questionnaire items do not directly measure the defining gap between rapid insight and slower linear explanation. The pattern remains in PRECEPTA but is not automatically inferred in the pilot.

## D-005 — User feedback target
Users are not asked to validate the 26 scores they just entered. They are asked whether the **higher-order PRECEPTA interpretation** fits their experience and whether an important pattern appears to be missing.
