# DYNAMOS voluntary research contribution — consent text v0.1

## Short form shown before donation

**Contribute this result to DYNAMOS research?**

Your questionnaire and interpretation were calculated locally in your browser. Nothing has been sent yet.

If you choose **Donate my result**, DYNAMOS will receive:

- your 26 dimension scores;
- the version numbers of the questionnaire, model, and derivation rules;
- which PRECEPTA patterns were shown to you;
- your structured recognition responses;
- your structured missing-pattern responses and any optional targeted follow-up answers.

DYNAMOS will **not ask for** your name, email address, account, employer, diagnosis, postal address, exact location, or free-text biography as part of this research contribution.

Your contribution is voluntary. DYNAMOS is exploratory, non-diagnostic, and not a validated psychometric instrument. Aggregated research statistics may be published publicly. Individual contribution rows will not be published.

A random withdrawal token will be generated on your device. Keep it if you want the option to request deletion of your contribution later. DYNAMOS cannot identify which record is yours without that token.

[ ] I am contributing voluntarily.  
[ ] I understand DYNAMOS is exploratory and non-diagnostic.  
[ ] I understand that no identity is requested as part of the research record.  
[ ] I understand that aggregate statistics may be published.  
[ ] I understand that I need my withdrawal token if I later want this contribution deleted.

**Donate my result**

## Design note

This text is a product/research design draft, not legal advice. Before public collection begins, review the final consent/privacy wording and the actual hosting/logging configuration together. The existing DYNAMOS ethics guidance requires voluntary, transparent use, informed consent, and confidential handling of completed profiles.
