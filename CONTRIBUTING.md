# Contributing to DYNAMOS

Thank you for your interest in contributing to DYNAMOS — the Dynamic Neuro-Operational Systems Model.

DYNAMOS is an open framework, but changes to the canonical model are intentionally more controlled than changes to documentation, examples, or website code. The goal is to make the project open to scrutiny and improvement without allowing the model specification to drift silently.

## 1. What can be contributed

Contributions are welcome in several areas:

- corrections to documentation;
- clearer explanations or examples;
- accessibility and usability improvements;
- translations and localization proposals;
- website and visualization improvements;
- research references and evidence relevant to the framework;
- validation methods and research designs;
- proposed changes to model dimensions, PRECEPTA, derivation rules, or other canonical model structures.

## 2. Canonical model vs. supporting material

The repository distinguishes between:

- `model/` — the canonical DYNAMOS specification;
- `docs/` — explanatory and practitioner-facing documentation;
- `research/` — evidence, provenance, references, and validation work;
- `src/` — website and software implementation;
- `examples/` — synthetic examples;
- `archive/` — historical or superseded material.

A change to wording in `docs/` is not automatically a change to the model.

Any change to files in `model/` is treated as a **model change** and must be explicitly identified as such.

## 3. Principles that contributions must preserve

Contributions to DYNAMOS should preserve the following core constraints:

1. No single dimension produces a trait.
2. Interpretations are conditional and context-sensitive.
3. The same surface pattern may arise from more than one underlying architecture.
4. Cost and sustainability must remain visible alongside capability.
5. Withdrawal must not be framed as failure.
6. Regulation, compensation, and masking strategies must not be framed as deficits.
7. Diagnostic labels are annotations, not organizing categories.
8. DYNAMOS must not be represented as a diagnostic or validated psychometric instrument unless future evidence justifies such a claim.
9. DYNAMOS must not be used to make employment, legal, clinical, grading, selection, exclusion, or accommodation decisions.
10. Personal or identifiable case material must not be added to the public repository without an explicit and documented basis for publication.

## 4. Proposing a change

For small documentation or code corrections, a pull request is sufficient.

For changes to the canonical model, please open an issue first. The proposal should explain:

- the part of the model affected;
- the problem or contradiction being addressed;
- the evidence, observation, or theoretical reason for the change;
- whether the change is additive, corrective, or breaking;
- which existing dimensions, PRECEPTA patterns, derivations, examples, or documentation would be affected.

Where possible, model changes should resolve a demonstrated ambiguity or representational gap rather than simply add new constructs.

## 5. Evidence and research contributions

DYNAMOS is currently a theoretical and reflective framework rather than a validated psychometric instrument.

Research contributions should distinguish clearly between:

- established findings from external literature;
- hypotheses proposed by DYNAMOS;
- exploratory observations;
- validation results;
- interpretation or speculation.

Please include full references or persistent identifiers where available.

## 6. Examples and privacy

Public examples should be synthetic or clearly documented composites unless explicit permission exists for publication.

Do not submit:

- names or identifying details of private individuals;
- email addresses, telephone numbers, addresses, account information, or similar personal data;
- private clinical or diagnostic information;
- identifiable workplace or educational case material without an appropriate publication basis.

## 7. Versioning

DYNAMOS uses semantic-style versioning for the canonical specification.

- **PATCH** — corrections that do not alter the meaning of the model.
- **MINOR** — backward-compatible additions or clarifications.
- **MAJOR** — changes that alter the architecture, interpretation rules, or compatibility of existing profiles.

A documentation-only change does not require a model version change.

Canonical model changes must also be recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 8. Licensing

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the licensing structure of this repository.

- DYNAMOS model content, PRECEPTA, documentation, model data, diagrams, and educational material: **CC BY-SA 4.0**
- Software code: **GPL-3.0-only**

Third-party material remains subject to its original license and must be clearly identified.

## 9. Tone and terminology

Use neutral, non-stigmatizing, non-diagnostic language.

Prefer descriptions of mechanisms, conditions, costs, interactions, and contexts over fixed judgments about people.

DYNAMOS is intended to support understanding, reflection, research, and system design — not categorization of human worth or ability.
