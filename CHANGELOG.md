# Changelog

All notable changes to the canonical DYNAMOS model will be documented in this file.

The changelog records changes to the **model specification**, not every website, formatting, documentation, or editorial update.

Versioning follows a semantic-style convention:

- **PATCH** — corrections that do not change model meaning;
- **MINOR** — backward-compatible additions or clarifications;
- **MAJOR** — architectural or interpretive changes that may affect compatibility.

## [2.0.0] — 2026-09-25

### Canonicalized

- Established DYNAMOS v2 as the current public specification.
- Defined **26 dimensions** across **8 architectural categories**.
- Established the **1–10 reflective scoring scale** with qualitative context.
- Established **32 PRECEPTA patterns** as the canonical Phenotypic Interface Layer.
- Classified the composite persona layer as **experimental**, rather than canonical.

### Architecture

The canonical DYNAMOS v2 categories are:

1. State Dynamics
2. Process & Attention Architecture
3. Meaning, Drive & Protection
4. Time Dynamics & Recovery
5. Coupling Load
6. Feedback Sensitivity
7. Integration Coherence
8. Sustainability & Context

### Clarified

- DYNAMOS is a systems-architecture reflection framework, not a personality test.
- DYNAMOS is non-diagnostic and is not a validated psychometric instrument.
- No single dimension produces a trait or PRECEPTA pattern.
- Interpretations are conditional, contextual, and combinatorial.
- The same observable pattern may arise from different underlying architectures.
- Cost, sustainability, time, and relational dynamics are first-order parts of the model.

## Historical development

### Prototype — 16-dimensional DYNAMOS

The original prototype contained 16 dimensions organized around state dynamics, attention/process architecture, meaning and drive, time/recovery, and environmental coupling.

Iterative contrastive analysis revealed recurring conflations that motivated later revisions, including:

- social vs. internal feedback sensitivity;
- conceptual vs. affective integration;
- regulation capacity vs. regulation sustainability;
- general entry cost vs. context-dependent activation;
- undifferentiated coupling load vs. channel-specific coupling costs.

These revisions led to the current 26-dimensional DYNAMOS v2 architecture.

Historical working documents may be retained in `archive/`, but they do not supersede the current files in `model/`.
